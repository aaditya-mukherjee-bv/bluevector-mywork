Frequently Asked Questions

How does ETH ensure our agency remains compliant with Title VI and ADA requirements?
ETH specifically addresses federal mandates by providing high-quality translations in 135+ languages, ensuring "meaningful access" for Limited English Proficiency (LEP) populations. Crucially, the AI translation system preserves document metadata and accessibility tags (such as those in tagged PDFs), ensuring that translated outputs remain Section 508 compliant and screen-reader friendly.
Can we maintain consistent terminology for highly specialized legal or medical documents?
Yes. ETH features Glossary Enforcement, a unique capability that allows your agency to upload approved terminology databases for specific domains (e.g., public health, judicial, or social services). The AI is then forced to use these specific terms, eliminating the risk of "hallucinated" or culturally insensitive translations in high-stakes documents.
Is our sensitive citizen data used to train public AI models?
No. Security is a cornerstone of the SnapApp™ architecture. When deployed within your agency's Google Cloud Platform (GCP) environment, your data remains yours. ETH uses "zero-retention" APIs, meaning your documents are encrypted in transit and at rest, and are never used by Google or third parties to train global machine learning models.
We often have 100+ page handbooks. Can the system handle large-scale batch processing?
Absolutely. ETH is built for "Enterprise Scale" and supports Batch Translation, allowing users to upload entire folders of documents simultaneously. It natively handles large files (exceeding 100 pages) without timing out, and it maintains the original layout, images, tables, and fonts exactly where they belong in the translated version.
Does the platform allow for human oversight before a document is published?
Yes. ETH utilizes a Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) workflow. While the AI performs the heavy lifting in seconds, the platform provides a side-by-side editor for your internal bilingual staff or professional editors to review, refine, and "sign off" on translations. This ensures the final output meets your agency's highest standards for public-facing communication.
